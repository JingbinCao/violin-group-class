First-Position Half-Step Map

Example: A string 2nd finger is C♯5; D string 2nd finger is F♯4. The numbers shown use the correct octave.

Scales & Modes

Click any card and it will play the full scale from low to high; no need to choose a key or step through levels.

Mode note:C, G, D, and A major often sound bright; a, e, and d minor often sound calmer and more mysterious. Dorian and Mixolydian are two different mode colors.
